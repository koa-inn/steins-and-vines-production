# Split Maker's Fee → Maker's Fee ($45) + Materials Fee ($5 w/ PST)

<objective>
The Maker's Fee ($50) is being split into two Zoho service items: Maker's Fee (MAKERS-FEE, $45) and Materials Fee (MAT-FEE, $5). The Materials Fee carries PST because it covers physical materials (corks, etc.). Zoho items are already created — this plan updates the website to fetch, display, inject, and tax both fees correctly.
</objective>

<background>
**Current state:** A single `_makersFeeItem` is fetched from `/api/services` by SKU `MAKERS-FEE`. It appears in:
- Per-kit breakdown (Kit supplies / Maker's Fee / Kit Total)
- Cart sidebar nested detail line
- Tax calculation (fee tax added to GST group)
- Promo discount calculation
- Server-side injection as a Zoho SO line item
- Kiosk hardcoded at $50

**Key insight:** The kit price from Zoho already includes the total fee ($50 → now $50 still, just split). So the per-kit breakdown math (`bSupplies = bPrice - totalFees`) stays correct as long as we subtract BOTH fees. The Materials Fee has its own `tax_percentage` and `tax_name` from Zoho (GST+PST), so tax grouping works automatically.
</background>
