<objective>
Build the pure, I/O-free helper library for Past Sales and its cache-key constants, test-first.

Purpose: Every rule the ledger and customer routes depend on (in-store classification, Vancouver day bucketing, tender labels, void-excluded totals, the D-12 allowlist) lives here as pure functions so it can be proven with unit tests before any route exists. Routes in 92-03 / 92-04 stay thin.
Output: `lib/kiosk-history.js`, four new `CACHE_KEYS` entries, `__tests__/kiosk-history-lib.test.js`.

ASSUMPTIONS (recorded per orchestrator, owner can override at review):
- A-01 Third source label: invoices that are neither in-store nor online get source 'other' (rendered "Other").
- A-02 Draft invoices: `statusLabel('draft')` returns 'Draft' (RESEARCH Pitfall 4 recommendation). The ledger shows "Draft"; the customer route (92-04) folds 'Draft' into 'Unpaid' so customer history keeps D-14's four labels. Drafts are NOT excluded from the ledger count/total; D-04 excludes only Void.
- A-03 `debitcard` maps to Card; any unknown `payment_mode` maps to Other.
- A-04 Day bucketing uses `created_time` converted to America/Vancouver; rows without `created_time` fall back to `date`.
</objective>
