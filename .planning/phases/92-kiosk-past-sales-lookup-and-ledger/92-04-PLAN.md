<objective>
Add the customer-history and invoice-detail routes to the Past Sales router.

Purpose: Server half of the "By customer" tab (D-06 to D-09) and of the tap-to-expand line items used by both tabs (D-15), with the same auth, allowlist and cache discipline as the ledger.
Output: two GET routes in `routes/kiosk-history.js` and two test files.

ASSUMPTIONS (recorded, owner can override at review):
- A-07 Walk-in response is HTTP 200 `{ walk_in: true, invoices: [] }` (orchestrator decision 4); the client shows the D-09 message.
- A-08 Load older: the client sends `before=<window_start of the previous response>`; the server returns `date_end = before - 1 day`, `date_start = before - 12 months`. Initial window is `date_start = today - 12 months`, `date_end = today + 1 day` (UTC-date pitfall). HISTORY_MAX_YEARS = 10: `has_older` is false once date_start reaches today - 120 months, and before earlier than that returns 400.
- A-09 Source tag for blank-reference AND all-digit-reference invoices needs payment notes (a blank ref may be a kiosk SO payment; an all-digit ref is usually an online checkout but may be a kiosk recipe sale written through pos-recipe.js:856, and the in-store note wins per D-01/D-07): one `GET /customerpayments?customer_id=X` chain (cap 2 pages of 200) per cache miss, only when the window contains at least one blank or all-digit reference invoice. If 92-03 runs in detail-fallback or none mode, no payments are fetched here: blank-reference invoices are tagged 'other' and all-digit ones 'online' (documented miss, approved at the 92-01 owner gate: SO-pay invoices show Other and numeric-ref recipe sales show Online in customer history).
- A-10 If KIOSK_CONTACT_ID is unset the route proceeds and logs a warning once per request; the deploy check in 92-08 confirms it is set on staging and production.
