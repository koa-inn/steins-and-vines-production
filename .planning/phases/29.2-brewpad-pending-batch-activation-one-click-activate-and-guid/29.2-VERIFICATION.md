## Gaps Summary

**One blocker gap (CR-01):** The detail pane Activate button success handler at `js/brewpad.js:2611` calls `renderBatchDetail(b)` with a bare batch object. `renderBatchDetail` expects `data = { batch, tasks, plato_readings }` and immediately dereferences `data.batch` (line 2270). Passing a bare batch means `data.batch` is `undefined` inside the re-render, producing a blank detail pane and setting `_detailBatchId = undefined` / `_currentBatchDetail = {}`. This corrupts the module's current-detail state, causing all subsequent operations on the open detail (Zoho SO linking, timeline, cache invalidation) to fail silently or target the wrong batch.

The fix is a one-character-class change: replace `renderBatchDetail(b)` with `renderBatchDetail(data)`. The variable `data` is the parameter of the enclosing `renderBatchDetail` function, so it is already in closure scope at line 2611.

**One quality gap (CR-02, not a goal blocker):** The Needs Scheduling optimistic lock is silently disabled because `last_updated` is not emitted by the Apps Script `needsScheduling` summary builder (`adminApi.gs:1733-1741`). The activation itself succeeds, but concurrent edits cannot be detected. The fix requires a one-line addition to `adminApi.gs`. This does not block activation from completing but is a data-integrity risk under concurrent use.

All other items (WR-01 through WR-07) are quality warnings that do not block the primary activation flows.
