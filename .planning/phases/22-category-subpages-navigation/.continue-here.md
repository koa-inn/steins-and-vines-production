  (.qty-input, flex:1 unconstrained on .hop-card), NOT the weight slider. Subpage
  sizing lives in catalog-subpage.css scoped to .product-card.subpage-card and never
  reaches hops cards. Mitigation: when a subpage fix "doesn't apply" to hops, check
  the selector scope — hops is body[data-page="hops"]/.hop-card, subpages are
  body.subpage-catalog/.product-card.subpage-card.

  CSS/UI fixes shipped without tests. If revisiting, consider backfilling.
