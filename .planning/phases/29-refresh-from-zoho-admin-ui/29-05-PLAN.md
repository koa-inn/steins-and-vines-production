Close gap CR-02 (the feature's primary purpose silently no-ops) plus warnings WR-01, WR-03, WR-04. After a successful refresh, the refreshed customer name must be VISIBLE in both surfaces and the Batches Sheet must stay internally consistent.

**Name-coherence approach chosen: derive and write customer_firstname/customer_lastname from the refreshed customer_name.** Rationale: `getCustomerDisplayName` prefers firstname/lastname (admin.js:36-41, brewpad.js:34-39) and all modern batches have those populated (kiosk + BrewPad create paths). Splitting is chosen because it keeps the Sheet's firstname/lastname columns populated and coherent with downstream consumers (e.g. the public batch greeting at adminApi.gs:1437-1438) rather than blanking them. The split must be applied identically in both files and patched into every in-memory object/cache that the refresh already touches.
