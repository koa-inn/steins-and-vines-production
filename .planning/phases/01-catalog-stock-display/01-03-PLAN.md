<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <what-built>Category filter cleanup (CAT-01, CAT-02) and stock overflow warning (STOCK-02) in the kiosk, plus verification that existing stock display (STOCK-01), out-of-stock warning (STOCK-04), and post-sale cache bust (D-09) are working.</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Visit https://staging.steinsandvines.ca/admin.html?tab=kiosk and log in. Hard-refresh (Cmd+Shift+R) to bust the service worker cache.

**Test 1 -- Category Filter (CAT-01, CAT-02):**
1. On the kiosk Sale tab, look at the category filter dropdown
2. Verify it shows real category names (e.g., Wine Kits, Beer Kits) -- NOT "goods" or "services"
3. If any products have no category_name in Zoho, verify an "Other" option appears at the bottom of the dropdown
4. Select "Other" -- verify only uncategorized products appear
5. Select a named category -- verify filtering works correctly
6. Check product cards for uncategorized items -- verify NO category badge is shown (not "Other", not "goods", just no badge)

**Test 2 -- Stock Overflow Warning (STOCK-02, D-01 through D-03):**
1. Find a product with limited stock (stock count > 0 shown on the card)
2. Add it to cart until the cart quantity would exceed the shown stock
3. Verify a confirm dialog appears with text like: "[Product Name]" -- only N in stock, cart would have M. Add anyway?
4. Click Cancel -- verify the item quantity does NOT increase
5. Add again to trigger the dialog -- click OK -- verify the quantity increases past stock
6. In the cart, use the + button to increase past stock -- verify the same dialog appears
7. In the cart, type a number larger than stock in the qty input, then press Enter or click away -- verify the dialog appears

**Test 3 -- Weight Items Exempt:**
1. If any weight items (sold by kg) are in the kiosk, add one
2. Enter a kg quantity -- verify NO overflow dialog appears (weight items are exempt)

**Test 4 -- Existing Out-of-Stock Warning (STOCK-04):**
1. Find an out-of-stock item (stock = 0, shown as "Out of stock")
2. Click to add it -- verify the EXISTING dialog appears: "[Name] is out of stock. Add to cart anyway (special order)?"
3. This should be the same dialog as before, unchanged

**Test 5 -- Stock Display (STOCK-01):**
1. Verify product cards show stock quantities (In stock / Low stock / Out of stock labels with numbers)
2. Confirm these match what Zoho shows for those products

**Test 6 -- Post-Sale Cache Bust (D-09) (optional, only if you want to test a real sale):**
1. Complete a test sale through the kiosk
2. After the sale completes, refresh the product grid
3. Verify stock levels updated to reflect the sale (may take a moment for Zoho to process)
  </how-to-verify>
