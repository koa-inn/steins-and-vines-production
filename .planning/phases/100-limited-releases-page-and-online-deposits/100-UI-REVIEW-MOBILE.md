# Phase 100 — Mobile UI Review: limited-releases.html

**Audited:** 2026-10-29 (after plan 100-15 polish, commit 329aa295)
**Baseline:** 100-UI-SPEC.md (approved) + mobile best practice
**Method:** Headless Chromium (Playwright 1.x, Node 24) with device emulation (isMobile, hasTouch, DPR 2–3) against the local static server `http://127.0.0.1:8767/`, plus code review of `js/modules/18-limited-releases.js`, `css/limited-releases.css`, `css/styles.css`. Third-party requests (Helcim, reCAPTCHA, GTM) were blocked. The cookie notice was forced open with `svConsent.open()` because `consent.js` suppresses it under automation.
**Viewports:** 320x568 (Playwright "iPhone SE" preset, first-gen), 375x667 (SE 2/3), 360x780, 390x844 (iPhone 14), 430x932 (14 Pro Max), 768x1024 (iPad mini), 844x390 (landscape).
**Screenshots and scripts:** `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-koa-dev-steins-and-vines-website/5e87277e-f3e1-4631-ac5d-23b295a008ec/scratchpad/mobile-audit/` (filenames are cited below; raw numbers are in `results.json`).

---

## Verdict

**Not ready for a phone-first launch: one blocker and two high-severity issues.** The content layout is in good shape on phones. There is no horizontal overflow at any width from 320 to 844px, inputs are 16px (no iOS zoom on focus), steppers are 44x44, the price and stepper row never wraps, and the SO numbers on the confirmation never break mid-number.

The **sticky reservation bar (D-10) does not work on any phone.** `<main>` keeps an animated transform, and that turns `<main>` into the containing block for the `position: fixed` bar. The bar ends up pinned to the bottom of `<main>` (about 13,200px down the page) instead of the bottom of the screen. jsdom tests cannot catch this, which is why it shipped. Once it is fixed, a second issue appears: the bar covers the footer at the end of the page.

The calendar links are 19px-tall tap targets stacked 9px apart.

Everything else is polish: medium and low items.

## Looks good (verified)

- **No horizontal overflow** at 320, 360, 375, 390, 430, 768 or 844 (landscape): `scrollWidth === innerWidth` everywhere. The only off-screen elements are the intentional skip link and honeypot at -9999px.
- **RQ27 Elemento hero crop** holds at every width: the left strip of supplier text is gone and the bottles and RJS logo are intact (`se375-bar-fixed.png`, `ip14-rq27-hero.png`). The image overflows its frame by design and is clipped by `overflow:hidden`.
- **Steppers** are exactly 44x44, with a 44px count between them so no accidental neighbour taps. **Price + stepper row** never wraps, even at 320 (254px footer: price 101 + stepper 132).
- **Spec chips** wrap cleanly in 2–3 rows with no stray separators. The **"N selected" badge** and 2px burgundy selected border render correctly.
- **Inputs are 16px** (no iOS focus zoom). `type`/`autocomplete`/`inputmode` are correct: name `text`/`name`, email `email`/`email`/`email`, phone `tel`/`tel`/`tel`. Fields are 47px tall.
- **Validation:** 14px messages directly under each field, and focus moves to the first invalid field (`lr-name`) on a failed submit (`*-form-errors.png`).
- **Pay button** is full width below 640 (238–337px x 45). The hint is directly under the button and hidden when enabled.
- **Basket rows** at < 480 stack the release month under the name, with a 103x44 Remove button.
- **Confirmation** at 360/375: each `SO-0000xx` renders as **one fragment** (`nowrap` works), the "SO SO-" duplication is gone, there is one left edge with the list inside the padding, a long email wraps without overflow, and focus lands on "Your deposit is paid" **within the viewport** (top 392 of 667) after a realistic pay-button-position success. The bar is hidden after confirmation.
- **Bar logic** (with fix #1 applied): hidden while any part of `#lr-reserve` is visible, so it never covers Pay; hidden during payment states and after confirmation; hidden at ≥ 768. On keyboard open with a focused form field the bar stays hidden because the reserve panel is intersecting. Safe-area: `viewport-fit=cover` is set and `padding-bottom: calc(16px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` is present (add the `, 0px` fallback for parity with the spec).
- **Anchor jumps** from the calendar land the card 64px below the sticky header (`scroll-padding-top` 142px), so nothing is hidden under the header (`ip14-anchor-jump.png`).
- **Images:** every content image has `width`/`height` attributes (no layout shift). The first hero is eager with `fetchpriority="high"` and everything else is `loading="lazy"` + `decoding="async"`. Bottle files are small (8–22KB).
- **Reduced motion:** the `main` slide-in drops to 0s, the stepper transition is off, the spinner is hidden, and the checkmark renders in its final state.
- **Stepper focus** shows the global 2px burgundy `:focus-visible` ring. Focus order is summary → "−" (skipped while disabled) → "+".
- **Typography scale** holds: h2s pinned to 20/700 Playfair, h1 clamp 28.8–33.2px on phones, body 16/1.5 inside cards, notes measure about 254–353px (fine for phones).

## Notes

- The Playwright `iPhone SE` preset is the 320x568 first-gen model; a separate 375x667 context was used for SE 2/3.
- The sticky bar root cause was confirmed by experiment: injecting `body[data-page="limited-releases"] main{animation:none}` moves the bar from top 6043 to top 590 in a 667px viewport. This is a CSS fact about transformed ancestors, so it reproduces in iOS Safari as well as Chromium.
- The HelcimPay iframe could not be loaded locally. Finding 8 comes from reading the code: `appendHelcimPayIframe` / `removeHelcimPayIframe` and `lrSetPaymentState`.
- No source files were edited and nothing was committed.
