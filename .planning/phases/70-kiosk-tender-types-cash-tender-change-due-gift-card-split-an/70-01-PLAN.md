Add a Cash tender to the kiosk sale flow. Staff take physical cash; the system books the full kiosk sale (Zoho invoice + a `payment_mode:'cash'` customerpayment, stock decrement, batch creation) with ZERO Helcim interaction, plus a client-side change-due calculator and gift-card + cash split. This is the simpler, lower-risk, PCI-free half of Phase 70 and ships independently before MOTO.

Purpose: Close the reconciliation gap where off-system cash sales bypass stock decrement and batch creation, without weakening the hardened kiosk money path.
Output: A `tender:'cash'` branch reusing the existing `/api/kiosk/sale` → `/confirm` pipeline (price anchoring, Phase 67 pre-charge assertion, idempotency lock) and a kiosk Cash button + change-due UI.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| kiosk client → /api/kiosk/sale + /confirm | staff-present device-token tier; client-supplied body (tender, tendered/change) is untrusted |
| middleware → Zoho Books | server-authoritative booking (grandTotal, cashApplied) crosses here |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-70-01 | Tampering | client claims tender:'cash' to skip a card charge | accept | Cash IS a manual staff action — the physical cash is the proof, same trust model as staff taking cash today; gated behind the existing staff-present device-token tier. No card charge exists to skip. |
| T-70-02 | Tampering/DoS | double-tap double-books a cash invoice/payment | mitigate | Cash flows through the SAME moneyPath.acquireIdempotencyLock on /sale (pos.js:336) and /confirm (pos.js:1035); NO special-case to bypass the lock for cash (Pitfall 4). |
| T-70-03 | Repudiation | cash confirm carrying a transaction_id triggers void-on-failure against a nonexistent Helcim txn | mitigate | Cash confirm sends NO transaction_id; verifyManualCharge guarded off for cash (no pollTerminalResult); the outer void block (pos.js:1587-1668) no-ops when body.transaction_id is absent. Asserted in test. |
| T-70-04 | Tampering | client-supplied tendered/change amount inflates or deflates the booked total | mitigate | Change-due is display-only; tendered/change are never sent; the server books ONLY its own re-resolved grandTotal / cashApplied (Pitfall 5), never a /sale-time or client value. |
| T-70-05 | Integrity | partial booking (invoice created, cash payment not) | mitigate | Cash payment write lives in the existing paymentChain whose .catch propagates so failures are surfaced; idempotency cache prevents silent duplicate on retry; same care as the card path. |
| T-70-SC | Tampering | dependency supply chain | accept (n/a) | This plan installs zero npm packages (RESEARCH Package Legitimacy Audit: not applicable). |
</threat_model>
