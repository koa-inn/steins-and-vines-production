<objective>
Two defects in the kiosk sale→confirm handoff, both in `routes/pos.js`:

**M-A3 — charge/invoice amount drift (roadmap SC#1).** The terminal is charged `terminal_amount` at the `sale` leg (`pos.js:542`, persisted to the pending-charge record at `:570-578`). The `confirm` leg then *independently recomputes* `grandTotal` from the catalog cache (`:862-863`, `:988`) and books THAT figure as the `customerpayment` (`:1052-1059`) — **never comparing it against the amount actually charged.** A catalog refresh between the two legs (the kiosk busts and reloads `zoho:kiosk-products` routinely) silently books a payment that is not the money that was taken. The pending-charge record already stores the true charged amount and is never consulted.

**H4 — the idempotency lock is not released on confirm failure (roadmap SC#2).** `runConfirm`'s outer `.catch` (`pos.js:1256-1320`) voids the charge and returns 502 but **never releases `confirmIdemKey`**. The lock lives for the full `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_TTL` (300s). Staff retry the same sale, the client sends the same key, and `acquireIdempotencyLock` returns `contention` → **409 "Confirm already in progress"** — for five minutes, mid-transaction, after the charge was already voided. Verified as a real, currently-open gap (the `sale` path releases on terminal-push failure at `:594-596`; the `confirm` path releases nowhere).

Purpose: Closes M-A3 / roadmap SC#1 and audit H4 / roadmap SC#2.
Output: A confirm leg that can only book what was actually captured, and that never strands staff behind its own lock.
</objective>

<locked_decisions>
**D-50-04 — the captured amount is the authority, and kiosk confirm is strict in BOTH directions.**

The check mirrors `checkout.js` (Phase 49): read back the real captured amount via `helcimLib.getCardTransactionById(txnId)` and compare it against `terminalApplied` with a ±$0.01 tolerance, BEFORE the Zoho invoice/customerpayment chain runs. On mismatch, throw a tagged error (`err.isCapturedAmountMismatch = true`) and let the EXISTING `runConfirm` catch block route it through `moneyPath.voidWithTimeout` → respond 402. No second void path.

Two deliberate differences from `checkout.js`:

1. **The kiosk rejects an OVER-capture too.** `checkout.js` logs an over-capture and proceeds (online, the customer chose the amount in the HelcimPay iframe — an overpayment is theirs to make). On the kiosk, **the server sets the charge amount itself**. If the card captured MORE than confirm is about to book, that is not a customer overpayment — it is our own catalog moving between the two legs and overcharging the customer. It must void and reject, not book quietly and keep the difference. So: any drift beyond ±$0.01 in either direction → void + 402.

2. **The readback is compared against `terminalApplied`, not `grandTotal`.** `terminalApplied` is the figure actually booked as the `creditcard` customerpayment (`:1052-1059`) and is what the terminal was charged (net of the gift-card portion). Comparing against `grandTotal` would produce a false mismatch on every split-tender sale.

Guard conditions: run the check only when `terminalApplied > 0` (a 100%-gift-card sale has no terminal charge to verify) and only AFTER `verifyManualCharge` has resolved (so `txnId` is a real Helcim id, never the literal `'manual-confirm'`).

Why not read the pending-charge record instead? It is a Redis mirror of what we *intended* to charge. `getCardTransactionById` is what Helcim *actually captured*. Only the latter can catch a divergence between our intent and the money. The pending record stays as reconcile's orphan sentinel; it is not the amount authority.

**D-50-03 — the idempotency lock is released on failure, EXCEPT when an unvoided charge is in play.**

Implement once, at the top of `runConfirm` (and mirrored in the sale path), with a `res.on('finish')` hook rather than by editing every `return res.status(4xx)` site — `runConfirm` has ~10 failure returns and any future one would silently reintroduce the bug.

Rule: on response finish, if `res.statusCode >= 400` **and** `res.locals.__keepIdemLock !== true`, call `cache.releaseLock(idemKey)`.

The exception is load-bearing and is the reason this is not a blanket release. When the void FAILED (`_voidFailed === true`, i.e. `voidTransaction` rejected — including 50-01's new unconfirmed-void rejection), the customer is still charged. Releasing the lock there would let staff retry and charge a SECOND time on top of a live first charge. So the void-failure branch (`pos.js:1303-1316`) sets `res.locals.__keepIdemLock = true`, and the lock stays held for its TTL — the retry is deliberately blocked while `needs_manual_review` is surfaced to staff. Fail closed.

Net behaviours:
- Confirm fails, charge voided successfully → lock RELEASED → retry re-acquires → staff can re-run the sale. (SC#2.)
- Confirm fails, void failed / unconfirmed → lock HELD → retry gets 409 → staff must resolve the live charge manually. (Correct: the alternative is a double charge.)
- Confirm succeeds (201) → lock NOT released; the cached receipt under the same key is the replay signal (unchanged behaviour).
</locked_decisions>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `zoho:kiosk-products` catalog cache → `runConfirm` pricing | The cache is mutable between the sale leg and the confirm leg (it is busted and refetched routinely, incl. by confirm itself at `:1211`). Confirm treats it as if it were frozen at sale time. This is the drift channel — and it needs no attacker. |
| Helcim (captured amount) → middleware | The only authority on what money actually moved. Currently never consulted by the kiosk confirm leg. |
| kiosk client → `POST /api/kiosk/sale/confirm` | Untrusted, retryable. `body.transaction_id` and `body.reference_number` are client-supplied and steer the void path. |
| Redis (idempotency lock) → staff retry ability | A lock that is never released is a self-inflicted denial of service on the money path, mid-sale. |
