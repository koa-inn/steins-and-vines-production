Build the sale-and-read half of the gift-card middleware: the issue route (cert-number uniqueness, zero-tax Zoho invoice + payment to the income account, void-Sheets-row-on-Zoho-failure), the next-number suggestion, and the balance lookup — all server-authoritative.
