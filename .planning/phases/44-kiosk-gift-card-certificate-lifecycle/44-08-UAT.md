## Gaps

### G-44-01 — Issuance & reload mark Zoho invoice paid without charging the card  [BLOCKING]
status: open
requirements: [GIFTCARD-01a (issue), GIFTCARD-01d (reload)]
surfaced_by: owner UAT (issued GC-000001 $50 on staging kiosk)
files: [zoho-middleware/routes/gift-cards.js (issue, reload), js/kiosk.js, js/admin.js, zoho-middleware/routes/pos.js (cart/checkout)]

**Observed:** Issuing GC-000001 ($50) wrote the sheet row correctly and showed a success
toast, but created INV-000124 in Zoho and immediately recorded a $50 `creditcard` payment
(deposited to Undeposited Funds) — with NO Helcim terminal charge ever happening. The card
holder never paid through the system, yet Zoho reported the invoice `paid`. The issue/reload
routes hard-record a `creditcard` customerpayment assuming the terminal was used, but the
44-06 modal never invokes the terminal. Nothing enters the kiosk cart.

**Why it matters:** Phantom revenue — Zoho overstates collected cash by the face value, and
no payment is actually taken. The reload route mirrors issue and has the identical defect.
(The redeem path is correct — it genuinely charges the terminal for the remainder.)

**Owner decision (2026-06-29): Cart + terminal checkout model.**
Issuing/reloading a gift card must add a gift-certificate line to the kiosk cart and be paid
through the normal terminal checkout flow (real Helcim charge); the certificate is
created/activated on payment SUCCESS — not pre-recorded as paid. This reuses the existing
redeem terminal path and produces ONE invoice (the cart's) rather than a standalone
pre-paid invoice.

**Implied rework:**
- js/kiosk.js + js/admin.js (D-08): issue/reload affordance adds a cart line instead of
  calling /issue|/reload directly + toast.
- zoho-middleware/routes/pos.js: on successful kiosk sale containing a gift-cert issue/reload
  line, call Apps Script issue_gift_card / reload_gift_card to activate the cert (post-payment).
- zoho-middleware/routes/gift-cards.js: issue/reload routes must stop self-creating the
  invoice + phantom creditcard payment; activation logic moves to (or is driven by) the
  paid-checkout flow. Preserve cert-number uniqueness (D-02), server-authoritative balance
  (D-05), zero-tax on the gift-cert line (D-03), and void-on-failure semantics.
- Decide cert lifecycle for the cart model: reserve cert number at add-to-cart vs activate at
  payment success; handle abandoned carts (no orphan active certs).
