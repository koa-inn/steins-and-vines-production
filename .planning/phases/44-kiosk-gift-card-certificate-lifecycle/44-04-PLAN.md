<objective>
Extend the v4.2-hardened kiosk money path (pos.js) to accept a gift card as a split tender: reduce the Helcim terminal charge by the applied amount, close the goods invoice with two customerpayments (terminal + gift-as-'others'), and decrement the gift-card balance LAST so any upstream failure voids cleanly with no phantom decrement. This is GIFTCARD-01c (redeem / partial / split tender) at the money-path tier. It must NOT weaken the existing terminal→invoice→payment→void-on-failure→idempotency behaviour.

Purpose: Redemption is a tender path (D-01) that integrates with — without weakening — the hardened money path; balances are server-authoritative and atomic (D-05); tax is untouched (D-03/R-03).
Output: pos.js split-tender extension in processSaleWithPrices + the confirm handler, and pos-gift-card.test.js.
</objective>
