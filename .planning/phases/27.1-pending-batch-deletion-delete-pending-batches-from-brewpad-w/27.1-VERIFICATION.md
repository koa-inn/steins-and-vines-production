**Test:** Click Delete on a pending row. Verify the custom confirm overlay appears (not a native browser alert), showing the batch ID, product name, and customer display name in the message. **Why human:** Visual DOM rendering of the custom overlay requires a browser session.

**Why human:** Event propagation stoppage verified only via interactive click testing.

#### 4. BrewPad Needs Scheduling — Delete Button Visibility

**Test:** Open BrewPad dashboard on the iPad (or browser). Confirm the Needs Scheduling section shows a Delete button on each row. Then navigate to the Batches tab and filter by Pending — confirm batch-list cards have NO Delete button.
**Expected:** Delete on Needs Scheduling rows only; Pending filter cards are clean tap-targets.

#### 5. BrewPad Delete — Bottom-Sheet Confirm

**Test:** Tap Delete on a Needs Scheduling row. Verify the danger bottom-sheet appears with "Delete" label styled in red/danger, showing batch ID + product + customer + irreversible warning.
**Expected:** `showConfirmSheet` bottom-sheet with danger button class and identifying copy; no native browser confirm.

#### 6. BrewPad Delete — Stale Row Cleared After Confirm

**Test:** Confirm deletion of a Needs Scheduling batch. Verify the row disappears from the dashboard immediately without a page reload (the WR-01 fix: `loadDashboard()` is called on success).
