<objective>
Close 73-REVIEW.md CR-01 (and the adjacent WR-01): the inventory stock gates still
compare a scaled recipe-line quantity, in the recipe line's own unit, directly against the
catalog item's `stock_on_hand`, which is in the item's own stocking unit — with NO unit
conversion. This is the exact bug class Phase 73 was commissioned to eliminate, left un-fixed
on the two `checkScaledStock` call sites (`pos-recipe.js:240` quote-time gate, `pos-recipe.js:665`
confirm-time gate) that 73-03 modified, plus the informational availability endpoint.

Concrete risk: a per-kg item stocked as `0.4` (kg) versus a recipe line needing `500 g`
computes `500 > 0.4 == true` (false block on a valid sale) OR, in the review's stated
magnitude direction, a `g`-stocked item at `400` vs a `0.5 kg` line computes `0.5 > 400 == false`
(no conflict → oversell of physical inventory). Both directions are wrong; the stock gate must
use the same unit-aware conversion the pricing sum-sites already use.

Purpose: quote/sale stock draw-down decisions become unit-aware and consistent with the
already-fixed pricing path; the availability badge stops mis-reporting.
Output: `checkScaledStock` and the availability handler route through the existing
`ingredientLineCost`/`classifyUnit` helpers; regression tests prove the mixed-unit cases.
</objective>
