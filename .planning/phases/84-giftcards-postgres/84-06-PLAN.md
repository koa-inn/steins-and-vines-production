| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-84-31 | Tampering | replay double-applies a write | mitigate | Replay reuses stored tx_ref verbatim; tx_ref UNIQUE makes it idempotent (84-01 proof) |

| T-84-34 | Denial of Service | Sentry alert flood from frequent health probes | mitigate | 10-minute in-process throttle on the DB-down alert |
| T-84-35 | Information Disclosure | staff email in pending-record logs | mitigate | Logs carry tx_ref/cert/amount/op only; full params only inside Redis |
