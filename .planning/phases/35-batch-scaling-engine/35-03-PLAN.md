## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-35-03-01 | Tampering | client-supplied target_volume_l drives price | mitigate | Server recomputes `scale_factor = target_volume_l / batch_size_l` and the full price via computeScaledRecipeTotal from locked_price/catalog rates at BOTH quote and confirm; client cannot lower price by choosing an odd volume |
| T-35-03-02 | Tampering | client supplies scale_factor directly | mitigate | Handler never reads `body.scale_factor` (asserted by grep gate); factor is always derived from target_volume_l server-side |
| T-35-03-03 | Tampering | override bypasses stock block | accept | D-08 design intent — override is an explicit staff escape hatch within the Google-OAuth-authenticated admin session; no extra crypto guard in scope; surfaced in UAT (Plan 04) |
| T-35-03-04 | Tampering | client sends scaled quantities at confirm to skew invoice | mitigate | Confirm re-fetches the recipe and re-scales server-side (Pitfall 1); client-supplied ingredient quantities are never used for line items |
| T-35-03-05 | Denial of service | absurd target_volume_l (fat-finger 600 L) | mitigate | Bounds check rejects `target_volume_l <= 0` and `> batch_size_l * 10` with 400 before any scaling/stock work (D-11) |
| T-35-03-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | mitigate | No package installs in this plan — pure wiring against existing deps. RESEARCH Package Legitimacy Audit: no new packages. Nothing to verify. |
