<objective>
Gap closure for the Phase 35 staging UAT defect: recipe stock checks read the purchasable-only ingredient catalog (`CACHE_KEYS.INGREDIENTS`), which deliberately excludes internal-only items. Recipe ingredients that are internal-only (e.g. "Gypsum (Calcium Sulfate) (Bulk)", item_id 109900000000028635, in stock 20.83 kg) are therefore invisible to the stock map and report stock 0 → the recipe falsely reads `cannot_brew` (blocking the kiosk sale) and Phase 35's D-08 scaled stock gate would falsely 409.

Fix: switch the stock/catalog reads to the FULL catalog `CACHE_KEYS.INGREDIENTS_ALL` (which includes internal items, with real stock_on_hand and rates) in the three stock-check sites. The full catalog is the correct source because recipe ingredients legitimately include internal/bulk items.
</objective>
