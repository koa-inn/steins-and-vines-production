How It Works — Step by Step
1
Customer pays at your iPad. You use the Helcim POS app as normal — find the product, hit charge, customer taps their card. Nothing changes about your in-store routine.
2
Helcim fires a webhook. The moment the payment goes through, Helcim sends a POST to your middleware with a transactionId. Your middleware already has this endpoint and verifies the signature. Already built
3
Middleware fetches the full transaction. Using the transactionId, it calls Helcim's API to get the amount, items, customer info, and card details. New code needed
4
Middleware syncs to Zoho. It creates (or finds) the customer contact, builds a sales invoice in Zoho Books marked as paid, and decrements stock in your Redis inventory ledger — then triggers a Zoho Inventory adjustment. New code needed
5
Everything is in sync. Your Zoho Books has the invoice, Zoho Inventory reflects the stock change, and your website product cache refreshes automatically. All within seconds of the card tap.
